Freiburg was a city I had wanted to see for a long time. When my friend suggested me to go to Basel, I felt this is the time. We decided to have a small-ish trip to Freiburg and Basel. 

We went from Tübingen to Freiburg with Flixbus and it was the best Flixbus I have ever been in. It had a very large legroom and no one was sitting near me. After Milan-Tübingen Flixbus, I swore that I would never get on Flixbus again, but this bus changed my mind a little.

After a 2,5 hours ride, we arrived Freiburg and left our bags in a bicycle shed of our AirBnB. Then we started walking to city center. We were going to met a friend in university library so we decided to take a tour inside the library too. It was way bigger than Tübingen’s and they had water stations (I still don’t know why we don’t have😭). Then we met with the friend and take a tour around the city. We ate lunch in a Korean restaurant. Then we climb to the cathedral’s tower.

The second day was set aside for hiking. We discussed about where to go while eating Flammenkuchen. After considering a few options, we decided to visit the Todnau Waterfall. We went back to our AirBnB to sleep after a long day.

Aside waterfall, we wanted to visit two places: Hasenhorn Rodelbahn and Blackforestline


Hasenhorn Rodelbahn is some kind of roller coaster which you use individual cars and adjust speed yourself. To go there, we used S-bahn from Freiburg Hbf to Kirschzarten, then Bus 140 from Kirschzarten to Todnau Busbahnhof. There are supermarkets around Todnau Busbahnhof so it was easy to get something to eat and drink. From Busbahnhof, we walked around 10 mins and arrived at the place where the chairlift departs.

The coaster was one of the best things I've ever did! It was so much fun! Since I could adjust my speed, it wasn't scary at all. I'd like to try again asap. It was 2.9km long and the speed limit was 40km/h.


Our next stop was Blackforestline. To go there, we took Bus 140 from Todnau Busbahnhof to Hangloch. We used kiosks to get our ticket.

Next morning, we went to Basel thanks to Deutschlandticket. I learned that there is a German train station in Basel which was interesting. 

I don’t have much to say about Basel. It was a “city”, expensive, too hot (very few trees inside the city). We joined a city tour, saw Basel Exhibition Center which had a very cool design, old town, Basel Münster, City Hall, Tinguely Fountain and Spalentor.

The thing I'd like to talk about is Basel Münster. The stairs going to the tower were too narrow and it was hard to pass even for one person. They should have warn people before going to tower because it was very claustrophobic. And the view was mid. The best thing we did was eating strawberries we brought from Freiburg in the courtyard of the cathedral. Then, we visited cartoon museum. It had a great library and we took some time to read cartoons there.
